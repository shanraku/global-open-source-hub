# Global Jobs discovery

The Global Jobs page is a free discovery layer inside Global Open Source Hub. It helps people search external job services using a role/skill keyword, location and work preference, and provides a curated starting list for remote, general, early-career, humanitarian, public-sector and freelance opportunities.

## Important scope and truthfulness

- This is **not** a global jobs database and does not crawl or aggregate every vacancy.
- Search links open independent providers. Their availability, coverage, ranking, sign-in requirements, fees and terms can change.
- Search results are not vetted or endorsed by this project. Applicants must verify a vacancy on the employer's official careers page.
- No account, payment, personal profile or CV upload is collected by this static page.
- The page does not promise a job, income, visa, interview or placement.
- Do not add automated scraping, bypass logins, collect personal data, or publish unverified job listings.
- Never charge job seekers to access this directory or apply through it.

## Safety and inclusion

Keep the no-pay-to-apply warning visible. Flag recruiter fees, identity theft, phishing, unpaid work presented as paid, misleading remote-location claims and discriminatory listings. Distinguish paid employment, internships, contract work and volunteering. Add local public employment services and language-specific sources through reviewed contributions.

## Future work

A genuine live multi-country vacancy aggregator would need reliable provider APIs or licensed feeds, source attribution, freshness timestamps, deduplication, country/work-authorisation filters, moderation, abuse reporting, and a privacy-conscious backend. Do not label results live or verified until those capabilities exist and are tested.

## Community opportunity submissions

Signed-in users can suggest job sources, internships, open-source projects, grants, learning resources and corrections through the Community Contributions form. Submissions are private to their owner and default to pending review. Users can see the status of their own submissions; they cannot publish, approve, edit or view another user's submission. Human review is required before any suggestion is added to public listings. Only HTTPS URLs are accepted, and the form must never request CVs, identity documents or other sensitive personal data.
