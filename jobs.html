STAY SAFE WHILE APPLYING

Real opportunities. Careful decisions.

  • Never pay a recruiter or employer to get a job, interview, offer letter or guaranteed placement.
  • Verify the vacancy on the employer's official careers page before sharing personal documents.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes, bank PINs or remote-access control with a recruiter.
  • Be cautious of unusually high pay, urgent pressure, fake checks, crypto deposits and requests to buy equipment from a specified seller.
  • Check work authorisation, visa rules, contract terms and whether an opportunity is paid or unpaid.
  • Report suspicious listings to the job platform and relevant local authorities.

This hub links to external sources; it does not verify, employ, recruit for or guarantee any listed vacancy.
